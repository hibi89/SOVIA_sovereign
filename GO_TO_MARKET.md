# Zero-cash business strategy and commercialization
2026-10-25 KST | Public-safe version | Prices below are hypotheses, not evidence of real sales.

## Operating thesis
Sell verifiable AI agent behavior evidence, practical policy review, and audit-ready reports. Do NOT sell generic "AI errors notify me" monitoring, guaranteed security, regulatory certificates or legal liability transfer.

## ICP and buying event
1. AI automation agencies shipping n8n/agent workflows to clients: need repeatable delivery proof. Priority 1 due channel multiplication.
2. SMBs operating tool-using agents without an AI security specialist: need understandable, bounded monitoring. Priority 2.
3. Confidential legal/accounting/medical firms with internal RAG: consider local Private once proven, require on-prem and specific logs. Do not assume they buy automatically.
Potential buyer: owner of agent delivery, technical lead or operations/security manager; decision must be validated.

## Offer ladder
Free: synthetic-risk audit demo, public checklist and no client data transmission.
Audit Lite: scoped **one-time** local review; price hypothesis 199,000 KRW for one agent or supported log source, capped records and fixed deliverables. No 24/7 monitoring.
Cloud Starter: hypothesis 79,000 KRW/month with quotas and self-service; Business 199,000 KRW/month. Set quotas, storage and alert limits before sale.
Private: separately quoted installation plus yearly maintenance after paying pilot.
Prices are provisional and should be adjusted from actual customer interviews, channel fees, taxes and delivery hours.

## Lowest-cash acquisition channels
- GitHub public product synopsis + safe demo code/docs when the owner approves publication.
- Documentation/tutorial searchable by actual pain: AI tool usage logs, n8n audit trail, unauthorized destination audit.
- Optional Krmong listing for limited-scope audit (channel, not primary business); do not purchase ads before organic proof.
- Asynchronous outreach to AI delivery agencies: email/DM with evidence-based demo, no unsolicited customer secret requests.
- Remote referrals/white-label resellers after evidence of repeatable setup.

## Execution and cash controls
Stage 0: current PC + Python, SQLite, static demo; no hosting spend and no third-party AI inference. Hosting and domain optional.
Stage 1: use no-cost local diagnostic, synthetic evidence, free static website only if useful.
Stage 2: paid Lite pilot funds fixed small overhead (payment/tax/legal/security expenses checked before service).
Stage 3: cloud purchased only after owner-approved budget and customer data governance.
No implied external data processing consent. Keep real client data local; require sanitized examples and a written scope.
Do not use pricing examples as projections or claim the first customer is acquired.

## Research and kill/pivot gates
Collect at least 10 customer discovery conversations (asynchronous acceptable), 3 detailed workflows, 1 willingness-to-pay or deposit/pilot signal before funded Cloud.
If interest but no money: change deliverable or buyer. If free observability solves customer needs: avoid cloning commodity features. If per-client customization dominates: productize an integration and keep remaining work as paid setup.
Metrics: lead->demo->pilot conversion, revenue, support hours per tenant, false positives, event cost, cancellation reasons.
Trademark: SOVIA name usage must be cleared in relevant jurisdictions before significant brand investment.
